Howausa: Bunny Budget Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Howausa: Bunny Budget ("the app") is made by an independent developer. Our principle is simple: your budget is yours. We can't see it, and we don't collect it.

1. Where your data lives

  • Your transactions, accounts, budgets, savings jars and receipt photos are stored on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync, the data is stored in your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). Only your Apple Account can access it; the developer cannot.
  • To keep your devices in sync, iCloud sends silent sync notifications. They only say that new data is available and never contain your records.
  • The summary shown in Home Screen widgets (such as today's spending) is passed from the app to the widget on your device and never leaves it.
  • The app has no servers of its own and no sign-up.

2. Shared ledgers

When you invite someone to a shared ledger, the ledger and your profile (nickname and photo) are shared with them through iCloud. You can stop sharing or remove people at any time.

3. Backups and exports

When you back up or export in Settings → Data, the file only goes where you choose (the Files app, iCloud Drive, or someone you send it to). The app does not upload it anywhere else.

4. What we don't do

  • No ads, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell or share your data.

5. Permissions

PermissionUsed for
Camera & PhotosAttaching receipts to transactions or setting a shared-ledger profile photo; photos stay on your device and in your iCloud
Location (while using)Saving where you spent, converted to a place name by Apple's location services; tapping a place opens Apple Maps
Face IDUnlocking your data; Face ID is handled by iOS and the app never receives face data
Motion & FitnessRequired by an animation component; the app does not read or collect motion data
NotificationsEntry reminders, recurring bills and repayment reminders, all scheduled on your device

You can turn off any permission in iPhone Settings at any time; only that feature stops working.

6. Network access

  • iCloud: sync and shared ledgers go through Apple's iCloud and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Exchange rates: with multiple currencies, the app downloads the latest rate table from a public service (open.er-api.com). No personal or budget data is sent (as with any internet connection, that server sees your IP address).
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Apple and we never receive your payment details. The app reads Apple-signed purchase records to check your plan. For the Couple plan, the purchase record is stored in your shared ledger so your partner's device can verify it.

7. Deleting data

Clear data in Settings → Data in the app; deleting the app removes data on your device. iCloud data can be removed in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We'll update this page and the date above; significant changes will be announced in the app.
