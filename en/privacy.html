Howausa: Bunny Budget Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Howausa: Bunny Budget ("the app") is made by an independent developer. Our principle is simple: your budget is yours. We can't see it, and we don't collect it. The only exception is the parts of Auto that you turn on yourself: personal info is masked on your phone before anything is sent, and you can see exactly what was sent. See section 4.

1. Where your data lives

  • Transactions, accounts, budgets, savings jars, receipt photos and other data are stored on your own phone (iPhone or Android).
  • If you turn on iCloud sync on iPhone, the data is stored in your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). Only your Apple Account can access it; the developer cannot.
  • To keep your devices in sync, iCloud sends silent sync notifications. They only say that new data is available and never contain your records.
  • If you turn on Google Drive sync (iPhone and Android), the data is stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder the app creates. The app only asks for permission to access files it created itself, so it cannot see anything else in your Drive; the developer cannot either.
  • When you sign in with Google, the app receives your email and name. They stay on your phone and are only used to show which account is signed in.
  • The summary shown in the Home Screen widget (iPhone), such as how much you spent today, is passed from the app to the widget on your phone and never leaves it.
  • No sign-up needed. Your ledger data is never sent to our servers; our server is only used for the Auto features in section 4.

2. Shared ledgers

When you invite someone to a shared ledger, the ledger and your profile (nickname and photo) are shared with them through the sync service you use (iCloud). You can stop sharing or remove people at any time.

3. Backups and exports

When you back up or export in Settings → Data, the file only goes where you choose (the Files app, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or someone you send it to). The app does not upload it anywhere else.

4. Auto (Beta)

The Auto tab turns notifications from banks, credit cards and payment apps into records waiting for you to confirm. Here is what is processed where, what is sent, and how long it is kept.

Processed only on your phone

  • How notifications come in: on iPhone, an automation you set up yourself in the Shortcuts app hands notifications to the app. On Android, the app reads them after you turn on Notification access, and only from the apps you check in "Apps to read".
  • The original notification text stays on this phone only. It is not synced to iCloud or Google Drive and is never uploaded. It is kept for 30 days by default (change it in Auto settings → Keep original text) and then deleted automatically; records you've already saved are not affected.
  • Masking personal info, matching against the reading rules, and reading the amount, store and time all happen on your phone. What gets masked: card numbers (all but the last 4 digits), account numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, URLs, Taiwan ID and resident certificate numbers, Japan's My Number, and the names of ledger members.
  • When you fix a misread notification, the wording Bunny learns is saved on this phone only (up to 200, not synced).
  • Which account a card's last 4 digits belong to: only the last 4 digits are saved, with the account, in your own iCloud or Google Drive, and they are never sent to our server.

Downloading the reading rules

  • The app downloads the latest reading rules from our server. No personal or financial data is sent when it does. The rules are signed by us, and the app checks the signature before using them.

Let AI help read (subscribers only; off until you tap "Agree")

  • Only notifications the reading rules can't read are sent. What is sent: the notification text with personal info masked, the time it arrived (with time zone), and your list of category names (so AI can suggest a category). The app shows exactly what was sent each time, and which AI it went to.
  • To check your plan, the app also sends the purchase record signed by Apple or Google Play (in the TestFlight beta, the install record signed by Apple). It contains no name, email or payment details.
  • The content is passed through our server to an AI service: Anthropic's Claude (USA) by default; if needed we may switch to DeepSeek (servers in China). The AI service processes the content under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • Our server does not store what was sent or what the AI read. To count the limit of 100 per person per month, it only keeps a code derived from your purchase number that can't be reversed, the month, and the count, and deletes them after the following month ends.
  • You can turn it off anytime in Auto settings → Let AI help read; once off, nothing is sent.

Teach Bunny (off by default; we ask you together with Let AI help read the first time)

  • For notifications the reading rules can't read, or that you corrected, the app sends us the "wording" to help make new rules. The wording is the text with numbers replaced by #, the amount, store and time replaced by markers, and personal info replaced by ●, plus the source app's name, whether it's income or expense, and the currency. The original text, amounts, store names and your name are never sent.
  • If you change a notification that AI read, we also send the wording as AI read it and which fields were different (for example amount, merchant or category), scrubbed the same way. Amounts, merchant names and category names before or after your change are never sent.
  • Each wording carries a ledger code: a hash of the ledger's internal ID, hashed again by our server, so no one can tell which ledger it is or whose. It's only used to count how many different ledgers show the same wording.
  • Each device sends at most 20 a day, and the same wording only once. Our server checks for anything that looks like a person's name or an address and rejects it. Accepted wordings are kept for up to one year (from when first received).
  • Our team reviews the wordings and uses Anthropic's Claude to help turn them into rules. The finished rules contain no personal info and go into the reading rules everyone downloads.
  • You can turn off Teach Bunny anytime; once off, nothing is sent (your phone still learns locally).

Misread reports (sent only when you tap "Send report")

  • When you tap "Send report" under "Misread?", the app sends the full notification text with personal info masked, plus what Bunny first read and what you changed it to (amount, time, store, category name, card's last 4 digits), and the AI's original reply or which reading rule was used. The app shows you exactly what will be sent first.
  • While Teach Bunny is on, a notification that AI said wasn't income or expense but that you saved anyway is reported with the same content.
  • Each report carries a device code the app generates at random and hashes, hashed again by our server. It's only used to count the limit of 10 per device per day and is deleted after 2 days.
  • Our team reviews reports and uses Anthropic's Claude to help turn them into new rules. Reports are deleted 30 days after they're handled, and unhandled ones after 90 days at most.

Our server

  • The Auto server runs on Cloudflare. As with any connection, Cloudflare sees your IP address; we don't record IP addresses or keep request logs.
  • The AI service only receives what our server forwards and never sees your IP address.

5. What we don't do

  • No ads, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • What Auto sends is only used to read that record and improve the reading rules, never for anything else.

7. Network access

  • iCloud: sync and shared ledgers go through Apple's iCloud and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Drive and Google sign-in: with Google Drive sync on, data travels directly between your phone and your own Google Drive, never through us, and is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Exchange rates: when you use multiple currencies, the app downloads the latest rate table from a public service (open.er-api.com). No personal or financial data is sent (as with any connection, that server sees your IP address).
  • Auto server: downloading the reading rules, Let AI help read, Teach Bunny and misread reports connect to our server on Cloudflare; what is sent and how long it is kept is in section 4.
  • AI services: Anthropic (Claude) or DeepSeek, which only receive the masked content described in section 4.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) and we never receive your payment details. The app reads Apple- or Google Play-signed purchase records to check your plan. For the Couple plan, the purchase record is placed in your shared ledger so your partner's phone can confirm the plan is active. When you use Let AI help read, this purchase record is also sent to our server to check your plan (section 4).

8. Deleting data

Clear data in Settings → Data in the app; deleting the app removes data on your phone. iCloud data can be removed in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage. For Google Drive, delete the app's folder in your Drive and remove the app's access under "Third-party apps & services" in your Google Account.

Auto's original notification text and learned wordings live only on your phone; in Auto settings you can shorten how long originals are kept and delete learned wordings, and deleting the app removes them too. We don't keep what was sent to AI. Wordings sent to us can't be traced back to you, so we can't pick out yours to delete, but they are deleted automatically within one year.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
