Howausa: Bunny Budget

A little felt bunny named Cotton lives in your phone and helps you track and save.

What Cotton can do

Log in a tap

Pick a category, type the amount, done. It shows up on Home right away.

Cotton's room

A sunlit afternoon journal by day, a rainy window by night.

Budgets you can see

One ring shows what's left. Overspent categories turn red.

Saving like a stamp card

Every time you save, you get a stamp.

Budget together

Share a ledger with your partner or roommate and see who logged what.

Home Screen widgets

Three widgets. Tap to log right from your Home Screen.

Travel in any currency

Make a travel ledger and foreign currency converts automatically.

Monthly recap

At the start of each month, see where last month's money went.

What's new

You can also see what changed in each version in the app under Settings → Help → Release Notes.

Build 4011/12
  • Swipe left on any recap card to pull out the month list and jump to any month.
  • Swipe right on a recap to go back home.
  • Settings now has a Monthly Recap list for all your past months.
Build 3911/12
  • Swipe up and down to change months and left and right to change cards in the Monthly Recap, following your finger.
  • Tap the left half of a recap card for the previous one, the right half for the next.
  • Swipe left on the last card for the month list, or right on the first card to go home.
Build 3811/12
  • New Monthly Recap: at the start of each month, see how much you spent, where it went and how much you saved.
  • A note on the home screen opens your latest recap.
  • In Settings, choose to compare "vs last month" or "vs usual".
Build 3711/12
  • Data import now supports CSV files exported from CWMoney and 天天記帳.
  • If an import goes wrong, tap "Having trouble?" to email the file to the developer.
  • Tapping the home screen widget now opens a new entry; you can turn this off in Edit Widget.
