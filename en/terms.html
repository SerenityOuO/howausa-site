1. No sign-up needed

Howausa has no accounts and no servers of mine. Your entries are stored on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud when you turn on iCloud sync.

2. You keep your own data

Because I cannot see your data, I also cannot recover it for you. Before deleting the app or switching phones, turn on iCloud sync or export a backup from Settings → Export Data.

3. Shared ledgers

  • A shared ledger belongs to the person who created it. If the creator stops sharing or deletes the ledger, it disappears for everyone else too.
  • Don't use a nickname or avatar that impersonates someone, or that is illegal, sexual, hateful, or infringes on anyone's rights.

  • Exchange rates come from a public rate service and may be delayed or differ from actual rates. The amount your bank, card issuer, or merchant charges is what counts.
  • Balances, budgets, charts, and monthly recaps are calculated from what you enter. They help you get organized and are not financial advice.

5. Import and export

  • When importing a CSV from another budgeting app, the format may not match. Export a backup before importing.
  • If an import goes wrong, you can tap "Having trouble?" to email me the file. I use that file only to fix the import feature and delete it once it's fixed.
